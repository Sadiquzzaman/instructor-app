## Full description

### English

> Instructor brings your school, coaching centre or IELTS centre to your phone. Students, teachers and school staff use the same account as on the Instructor website.
>
> **For students**
> • See today's classes, your routine and the calendar
> • Take tests on your phone and see your results when they are published
> • Hand in homework with photos or files
> • Check in to in-person classes and see your attendance
> • Ask for leave, read class resources and chat with your teachers
> • IELTS: practise Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking, and follow your bands
>
> **For teachers**
> • Take attendance, set homework and mark it
> • Make tests, watch them live and publish results
> • Keep marks, behaviour notes and report cards
> • Talk to your classes and to parents in chat
> • IELTS: build tests, open them for batches, and mark Writing and Speaking
>
> **For school owners and admins**
> • An overview of the whole school: people, classes, subjects and sessions
> • Attendance settings, holidays and branches
> • Reports on attendance and performance
>
> The app works in Bangla and English, shows times in your school's time zone, and works on phones and tablets.
>
> You need an account from your school or centre to sign in.
