# Instructor Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [date of approval]**

Instructor ("we", "us") runs the Instructor website (instructor.academy) and the Instructor app for Android and iOS. Schools, coaching centres, IELTS centres and independent teachers ("organisations") use Instructor to run classes, tests, homework, attendance and results. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

**Contact:** [support email], [postal address of the company]

## 1. Who controls your data

When an organisation adds you (as a student, teacher or staff member), that organisation decides what happens with its records, such as your class, marks and attendance. We store and process them for the organisation. If you use Instructor as an independent teacher, or you sign up yourself, we control your account data.

## 2. What we collect

**Account information:** your name, phone number and/or email address, your role (student, teacher, owner and so on) and the organisations you belong to. We keep your password only as a secure hash, never the password itself.

**What you and your organisation add:** classes, routines, test questions and your answers, homework and the files or photos you hand in, marks, behaviour notes, report cards, leave requests, attendance records, class resources, and chat messages.

**IELTS:** your target bands and test date, your answers, and recordings of your Speaking answers.

**Location (only when you check in):** when you tap "Check in" for an in-person class, the app reads your phone's location once and sends it with the check-in. That way your organisation can confirm you were at school. The app never reads your location in the background or at any other time.

**Camera, photos, files and microphone:** only when you choose to take a photo, pick a file, or record a Speaking answer. Only what you choose is uploaded.

**Your devices:** the phone's make and model (for example "Google Pixel 8") and the app's version. We show them in "Your devices" so you can sign out a phone you no longer use. We do not collect advertising IDs.

**What we do not collect:** we do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services in the app, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 3. Why we use it

- To run the service: show your classes, tests, results and messages, and let your organisation manage them.
- To keep your account secure: sign-in, signing out lost devices, and preventing abuse.
- To send you service messages, such as one-time sign-in codes.
- To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who can see it

- **Your organisation's people,** according to their role. For example, your teachers see your answers and marks, classmates may see your name in a class, and owners and admins see school-wide records.
- **Our service providers,** who host and run the service for us under contract: [hosting company, country]; [SMS provider for one-time codes]. They may not use the data for anything else.
- **Authorities,** when the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account or your organisation's workspace is active. **[Retention period after an organisation leaves, e.g. 90 days, then deleted or anonymised.]** Records that an organisation must keep (for example exam results and attendance) stay with that organisation, as described in §6.

## 6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (**Account → Delete account**) or on the web at **[deletion page URL]**. Deleting your account:
- signs you out on every device;
- replaces your name, phone number and email with anonymous placeholders, so you can no longer be identified;
- keeps the records your organisation needs, such as exam results and attendance, under the anonymous name.

To protect you, we ask for your password or a one-time code first. If you own an organisation, you need to hand it over to someone else or close it first.

## 7. Children

Organisations may add students under 13. In that case the organisation is responsible for getting consent from a parent or guardian, as its local law requires. Children do not create accounts themselves in the app. A parent or guardian who wants to see, correct or delete a child's data can contact the child's organisation or us at [support email].

## 8. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage. Access to data on our side is limited to staff who need it.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or ask questions about this policy, at [support email]. For records your organisation controls, we may pass your request to that organisation.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the change is important, we will tell you in the app or by email.
